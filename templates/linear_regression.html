Linear Regression Application

Problem Description

The goal is to predict NVIDIA stock closing price based on historical data.

Dataset Explanation

The dataset contains NVIDIA stock prices from 1999 to 2026 including open, high, low, close and volume.

Variables Used

Input: Time (days)

Output: Closing price

Data Preparation

The dataset was cleaned by removing missing values and selecting the required columns.

Regression Graph

The model shows the trend of stock prices over time.

Interpretation of Results

The results show that stock prices have an increasing trend over time, although the model does not capture volatility.
